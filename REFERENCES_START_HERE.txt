QUICK START GUIDE
================================================================================

For Microsoft Word:
1. Open: docs/REFERENCES.md
2. Copy the references you need
3. Paste into your bibliography section
4. Done! (Already in APA format)

For LaTeX/Overleaf:
1. Upload: docs/references.bib to your project
2. Add to your .tex file:
   \usepackage[style=apa,backend=biber]{biblatex}
   \addbibresource{references.bib}
3. Cite using: \textcite{khonji2013}
4. Add bibliography: \printbibliography

For Zotero/Mendeley/EndNote:
1. Import: docs/references.bib
2. Use your reference manager's Word plugin
3. Insert citations as needed
